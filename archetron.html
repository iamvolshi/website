Architecture for
accountable intelligence.

ARCHETRON connects what a system knows, remembers, decides, is authorized to do, actually does, and can later account for.

Evidence→State→Cognition→Authority→Action→Observation

Persistence

State and history remain available to future decisions instead of disappearing between interactions.

Governance

Authority and policy define what the system may do, when, and under whose control.

Observability

Actions and outcomes remain visible enough to reconstruct what happened and why.
