LEGAL & REGULATORY GROUNDWORK
verified_user PATENT PENDING
FILED OCTOBER 23, 2026

U.S. Provisional Patent Application No. 64/182,709

Inventor: Darren Patrick Hall · The filing covers eleven mechanisms:

01 The sealed record
expand_more
every belief append-only, hash-chained to the entry before it. Tampering, deletion, or a broken interval is detected by verification, which identifies the exact affected row.
02 Knowledge time
expand_more
a third clock: not when an event happened, and not when it arrived, but when it was known, and at what authority.
03 Authority that falls without erasing
expand_more
a wrong belief remains in the record while its authority collapses. The system learns without rewriting its history.
04 Born registered
expand_more
every kernel is assigned a Kernel ID at birth. No KID, no read, no write. What is not yet understood runs as null: registered, present, and unassigned — never invisible.
05 The poison proof
expand_more
the record verifies its own integrity against tampering, deletion, and interval attacks, and produces proof of each catch.
06 The firewall
expand_more
each source carries a damage accumulator. Past a threshold, the source continues to be recorded in full and ceases to have any effect. The attack is preserved as evidence.
07 The self-healing loop
expand_more
the machine reads its own record, identifies a deficiency, constructs the missing component, and integrates it.
08 Parameters as deeds
expand_more
a change to a kernel's parameters is recorded as a versioned event on the same identity. No component receives a new identity to escape its history.
09 Snapshots, not state
expand_more
any view of the present is derived from the ledger and can be re-derived identically at any time. Where a snapshot and the ledger disagree, the ledger governs.
10 Pressure-pot attention
expand_more
influence the system cannot consolidate accumulates per kind until it crosses an attention threshold. Attention discharges it.
11 Debt and the curve
expand_more
outstanding deficit removes options from the injured component until it heals; a component's position on the response curve is set by design, as protection, as a sensor, or for deliberate retention.
